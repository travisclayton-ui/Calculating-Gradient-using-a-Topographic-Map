Bermagui Gradient Topographic Quiz

This PAT-style prototype uses a simplified Bermagui topographic map crop to test contour interpretation, steepness and gradient calculation. Based on the Bermagui topographic map, the scale is 1:25,000 and the contour interval is 10 metres.

Questions

1. What is the scale of this topographic map?

2. What is the contour interval on this map?

3. True or false: Contour lines that are close together show a steeper slope.

4. True or false: Widely spaced contour lines show a very steep slope.

5. Which statement about the A–B slope is correct?

6. Point A is on the 40 m contour and Point B is on the 100 m contour. What is the vertical difference between the two points?

7. The ground distance between Point A and Point B is 600 m. What is the gradient between A and B?

8. Which statement best explains why one slope is steeper than another on a topographic map?
